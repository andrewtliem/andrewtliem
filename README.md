## 🧭 About Me

I am a Computer Science educator and researcher at **Universitas Klabat**, working at the intersection of:

- **AI-native software engineering**
- **Agentic AI systems**
- **Computer Science education**
- **Intelligent optical networks**
- **Passive Optical Networks / NG-EPON**
- **Software-defined and AI-assisted network management**

My work combines academic research with practical tool-building. I am especially interested in how AI agents can support reasoning-heavy workflows: from optical network telemetry analysis to classroom feedback, academic writing support, and student software engineering education.

A simple principle guides much of my current work:

> **Delegate tasks, not judgment.**

AI can help draft, analyze, implement, and review — but humans remain responsible for intent, ethics, verification, and explanation.

## 🧰 AI-Native Education & Builder Projects

### 🧑‍💻 AI-Native App Builder Skills

A student-friendly skill pack for teaching students how to build apps with AI while preserving human judgment, verification, and traceability.

**Focus:**

- idea → intent → PRD → architecture → issues
- prompt engineering as software process
- AI-assisted build loops
- code review and testing
- traceability and demo evidence
- postmortem and reflection

Repository:

- [`ai-native-app-builder-skills`](https://github.com/andrewtliem/ai-native-app-builder-skills)

### 🧠 Lega — Personal Knowledge OS

Lega is an AI-powered personal knowledge system for cleaning, organizing, and connecting notes, PDFs, and ideas automatically.

**Goal:** help people turn scattered knowledge into structured, searchable, and connected personal intelligence.

### 🤖 Hermes Agent Workflows

I use and extend Hermes Agent as a practical environment for:

- local AI automation
- tool-using agents
- browser workflows
- student-support utilities
- AI-native software experiments

### ✍️ Scholarly Voice LoRA

A LoRA-based academic writing assistant focused on scholarly revision and style improvement — not simply paraphrasing.

**Goal:** help academic writers improve clarity, tone, structure, and scholarly expression while preserving their original meaning.

### 📄 PDF and Document Tools

I build small AI-native tools for document workflows, including PDF processing, academic revision, and teaching-material preparation.
